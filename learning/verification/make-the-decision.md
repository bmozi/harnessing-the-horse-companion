# Lesson 4: Choose ship, revise, or stop

Bring the scope, observations, recovery needs, and unresolved limits into one reviewable decision.

## Finish with a decision someone can inspect

Your record now contains a claim, a check, an observation, and a challenge. Use it to decide what the result earns. A correct local exercise is not proof of production reliability, and a good quiz score does not grant deployment authority.

For the CSV example, a reversible local export may be ready for its intended use once its agreed checks pass. A change to an externally consumed export may also need a compatibility check and a recovery plan. The required evidence follows the consequence.

## Compare your work

SHIP means the evidence supports the stated use within the owner's authority. REVISE means there is a specific repair or missing check to address. STOP means the boundary, consequence, or uncertainty prevents continuing under the current conditions.

Retain your record. In another session, apply the method to a different task without copying this example's acceptance criteria. Transfer is a better learning check than recognizing the same question again.

## Retrieval check

A required recovery check has not run. What should the completion report do?

1. Count the planned check as passing.
2. Omit it because the other checks passed.
3. State that it is unrun and explain its effect on the decision.
4. Replace its result with the agent’s confidence.

<details>
<summary>Check your answer</summary>

Answer: 3. An unrun check remains unrun. Its consequence belongs in the decision record rather than being hidden behind an otherwise green report.

</details>
