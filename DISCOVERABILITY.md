# Public discovery and citation practice

Use these practices for material intentionally published in this companion and
on its public learning site. They do not authorize exposing private manuscripts,
operational data or restricted resources, changing training permissions, or
relicensing existing material.

A useful public explanation answers a real question, explains the mechanism,
shows an example, names use cases and limits, and links to evidence or maintained
practice materials. Preserve constructed examples, unrun checks and unknown
outcomes as such. Do not invent experience, results, reviews or authority.

Identify the author, title, actual revision or update, appropriate license,
sources and correction route. Keep one canonical learning source in
[learning/pilot.json](learning/pilot.json); exported Markdown and the website
record their relationship to it. The books remain independently useful and
private manuscript chapters are not reproduced here.

For a website, verify public access, readable rendered text, canonical URLs,
metadata, internal links, required assets, and public-only crawl/sitemap scope.
Structured data describes visible content. An optional llms.txt or citation file
is a convenience, not a special indexing or ranking requirement. Authentication,
not robots.txt alone, protects restricted content.

Measure public access, actual indexing, observed AI citations, reader usefulness,
learning and commercial outcomes separately. Do not infer gains from a passing
technical check. Retain date, question, assistant/provider, settings and actual
cited URL when making a citation observation. No recurring measurement is implied.

- [Google: robots.txt introduction](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots/intro)

For attribution use [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff). Report a correction through this
repository's [Issues](https://github.com/bmozi/harnessing-the-horse-companion/issues)
with safe, reproducible examples rather than private records.
