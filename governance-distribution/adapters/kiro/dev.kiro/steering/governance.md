# Example Organization Engineering Baseline

- Establish acceptance criteria and prohibited changes before generation.
- Verify security-sensitive and architecture-sensitive claims against primary source.
- Prefer sandbox and no-op behavior as the default for operations with external side effects.
- Run applicable quality gates and disclose anything skipped.
- Use adversarial review before presenting a change as pull-request ready.
- Team steering may add requirements but may not weaken these rules.
