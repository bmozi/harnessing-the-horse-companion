## Current Release

The canonical companion for the revised first edition is **v2.0.0**,
released August 16, 2026. The files on the `main` branch may receive later
corrections; use the tagged release when you need a stable classroom or
team reference.

Earlier copies described fourteen standards. No control was removed in
the revised framework: structured generation became a practice within
Standard 1, and escalation and override handling became the
governed-exception half of Standard 6.

Found a mismatch? Check [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md), then use the
[errata issue form](https://github.com/bmozi/harnessing-the-horse-companion/issues/new?template=errata.yml).
