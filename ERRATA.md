## Reporting a Possible Error

Use the [errata issue form](https://github.com/bmozi/harnessing-the-horse-companion/issues/new?template=errata.yml)
and include the format or companion version, location, current wording or
behavior, and proposed correction. Do not post private company data,
credentials, or unpublished manuscript material.

Reports are reviewed before they appear here. Minor repository defects may
be fixed directly; changes that affect the book's meaning will be recorded
with the affected format and release.
