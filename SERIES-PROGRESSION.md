# The Accountable AI Engineering Series Progression

The four books answer different questions. No companion repository replaces
the argument, stories, tradeoffs, or judgment of its book.

| Book | The question it answers | What completion looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Book 1 — *The Amplified Engineer*** | How do I use AI without outsourcing judgment, quality, learning, or professional responsibility? | An engineer can direct AI as a thought partner, inspect its work, and remain accountable for the result. |
| **Book 2 — *Harnessing the Horse*** | How does a software team determine whether one AI-assisted change is ready to ship? | One team can move from request to specification, bounded execution, adversarial review, evidence, and a **SHIP / REVISE / STOP** decision. |
| **Book 3 — *The Accountable AI Software Factory*** | How do we make those disciplines durable across agents, teams, releases, side effects, and business outcomes? | The organization can reconstruct what was authorized, what happened, what evidence justified release, who owned the result, and what changed afterward. |
| **Book 4 — *The Adaptive Enterprise*** | How must the organization itself change so AI-enabled capability earns adoption rather than remaining a technical island? | Structure, decision rights, incentives, information flow, and customer value reinforce the new way of working. |

## The Book 2-to-Book 3 handoff

Book 2 is the practical entry point. Begin with one team, one repository, one
bounded change, and one honest decision. Move to Book 3 when any of these become
true:

- multiple teams or agents can affect the same outcome;
- work must survive across sessions, queues, or service boundaries;
- a side effect may occur even when its response is lost;
- policy, release, and outcome decisions belong to different authorities;
- evidence must remain bound to an exact artifact over time; or
- operational learning can change what the system is allowed to do next.

At that point, adding more prompts is not enough. Continue with *The
Accountable AI Software Factory* and its
[minimum accountable factory laboratory](https://github.com/bmozi/accountable-ai-software-factory-companion).
